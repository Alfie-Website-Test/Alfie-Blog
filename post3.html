US Midterms - Is Trump Feeling Blue?

Published: November 5, 2026

After a whirlwind US midterm election, the results are in. Texas is Blue. Boundaries are breaking. But is this all a disaster in the making?

Since the Democrats have control of the house and the senate (come Jan 2027), they will be able to put forward their own legislation. This will likely see attempts to ease the soaring cost of living, as well as reduce the reach of the authoritarian state that Tump has been sculpting with ICE.

But the President has a trump card that he has already made clear he is looking forward to playing. The Presidential Veto.

This is an important cog in the American machine of checks and balances, but is likely to be weaponised by a sulking Trump. While the Democrats did gain unprecedented victories geographically, winning Texas, Kansas, and Maine, they didn't incapacitate the Republicans numerically; falling short of a 2/3rds majority.

Lacking this, it means that they will have to work hard to fight filibustering and delay tactics from the GOP, and they won't be able to overturn any Presidential Vetoes. This could mean that the US is about to slow down and the Republicans are going to drag their heels since they haven't got what they wanted.

Trump's presidencies have seen some of the longest government shutdowns in US history. 2025 saw 43 days of inaction as an agreement on healthcare funding couldn't be reached. This shows that Republicans aren't afraid of throwing their toys out the pram. Trump has made it clear that he intends to use his veto to bully the Democrats into submission. This leaves me wondering if the final two years of Trump's America be his least productive? Only time will tell...
